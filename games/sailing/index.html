Windward

Keelboat racing, simulated properly. Apparent wind, sail aerodynamics, gusts, shifts and dirty air. One lap of a windward–leeward course against four rivals.

How to sail it
  • Left thumb is the tiller. Drag across to steer; let go and it centres.
  • Right thumb is the mainsheet. Drag up to trim in, down to ease out. It stays where you put it.
  • You cannot sail straight at the wind. The red wedge on the dial is the no-go zone — zig-zag through it, and the green marks are the angles that pay.
  • Watch the telltale by your right thumb. Luffing means trim in. Stalled means ease out.
  • Coming out of a tack, bear away a few degrees to build speed before you point up again.
  • New to it? Set the sheet to Assisted and the boat will trim itself while you concentrate on steering.
  • Keyboard: arrows or WASD.
