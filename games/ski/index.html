    Fastest getaways

      How to ride
      • Drag left or right anywhere to carve. Let go to run straight.
      • Drag down to tuck for speed. Drag up to dig in and brake.
      • Tap to jump — or tap a second finger while you steer.
      • In the air, drag sideways to spin, down to front flip, up to backflip. Land with your skis pointing down the hill and your head up.
      • They follow your line, so skim trees and rocks close — they cut corners and clip them.
      • They copy your jumps and your tricks. Spin a 360 and they must spin one too. Not everyone can.
      • Each run you get a new mountain and three new chasers, each with their own weakness. Watch the roster at the start.
      • Keyboard: arrows or WASD, space to jump.
