    How to ride
    • The screen is two ski poles, crossed over: hold the right side to turn left, the left side to turn right. Slide your thumb outward for a sharp turn.
    • Hold both sides to crouch and pick up speed. Let go to jump.
    • Tap left, right, left to skate when you are slow. Drag down on a side to brake.
    • In the air, hold one side to spin, drag both up for a backflip or both down for a front flip. Land with your skis pointing down the hill and your head up.
    • They follow your line, so skim trees and rocks close — they cut corners and clip them.
    • They copy your jumps and your tricks. Spin a 360 and they must spin one too. Not everyone can.
    • Each one you knock down gets back up once. Crash them a second time and they are out.
    • Each run you get a new mountain and three chasers with random strengths and a weakness each.
    • Keyboard: arrows to turn, down to crouch (let go or space to jump), up to brake.
